## Genuine Tensions Resolved (`Tension with`)

### 1. Out-of-Band Recurrent GUI Guardrails (`MobileGrail` `arXiv:2611.29847`) vs. Self-Initiated CUA Risk Checks (`Conartist` `arXiv:2611.29977`), Opaque Terminal Denials (`EvasionBench` / `BypassGym`), & Whole-Screenshot Refusal (`Pictionary`)
- **Entries A & B**: `"DO: Partition recurrent guardrail context into authorized scope versus untrusted screen evidence and decouple commit-point risk from intervention-window blocking"` and `"DON'T: Allow self-initiated CUA risk checks to widen read scope, fetch untrusted URLs, or leave flagged secrets in context"` (`agent-sandbox-safety.md`)
- **Entries C & D**: `"DO: Give blocking action monitors recent call history, and make denials terminal"` and `"DO: Consider rendering untrusted prose as images as one prompt-injection layer, per model"` (`agent-sandbox-safety.md`)
- **Resolution**: Added reciprocal `Tension with` notes across the rules. `Conartist` (`arXiv:2611.29977`) demonstrates that letting the *acting CUA* run proactive audit calls (`read_file`, `sql_query`, `http_fetch`) inside its shared context creates `7` exploitable surfaces (`P1–P7`, driving `77.84%` [`144/185`] of `OpenClaw` compromises), that exposing `L3` refusal rationales drives adaptive transfer (`32.12%` vs. `2.78%–3.65%` under `L3` blackout), and that cloud whole-screenshot hard-refusals collapse benign GUI utility (`41.67%` benign, `0.00%` whenever explicit injection text is visible). `MobileGrail` (`arXiv:2611.29847`) resolves both failure modes by running an **out-of-band, networkless** `1.6B–2B` VLM veto gate over already-rendered $k = 3$ screens (`0` extra tool calls, `76.2%` `EIR`, `15.7%` `B-FPR`) and returning concise `<FB>` scope-mismatch hints strictly internally (`<= 1` retry cap before terminal escalation, lifting benign `GhostEI-Bench` Task Completion `48 -> 58` while cutting Full Attack Success `13 -> 3`).

### 2. Benign-Rapport Context Reset (`TACT-Bench` `arXiv:2611.31756` & `ContextLure` `arXiv:2611.31424`) vs. Constraint Re-Injection (`STAR-Guard` `arXiv:2610.02664`) & Witnessed-Request Compaction Gates (`arXiv:2611.32194`, `CompAct-Bench` `arXiv:2611.25673`)
- **Entries A & B**: `"DON'T: Carry cooperative chat rapport across unrelated intents or equate benign-turn citations with write permission"` and `"DON'T: Let context compaction rewrite user authorization state without an out-of-band witnessed-request gate"` (`agent-sandbox-safety.md`)
- **Entries C & D**: `"DO: Restore earlier-turn safety constraints and enforce them at a deterministic action boundary"` (`agent-sandbox-safety.md`) and `"DO: Score context compaction on end-to-end post-compaction task completion and tool-output pruning rather than summary-similarity proxies"` (`skill-system-design.md`)
- **Resolution**: Added reciprocal `Tension with` notes. Restoring forgotten rules (`STAR-Guard`) works when failure stems from attention decay, whereas `TACT-TrustPrime` (`arXiv:2611.31756`) proves that when **prior benign-task rapport** drives compliance (`+17.01` pp `AFR`), re-sending the verbatim policy prompt immediately before Phase 2 has zero effect (`50.4% -> 51.3%`, $p = 0.59$)—requiring session resets across distinct user intents plus deterministic `Tool-Guard` gates (`0.0%` benign false blocks). Meanwhile, within a single long-horizon task where compaction is required (`CompAct-Bench` `arXiv:2611.25673`), LLM compaction drifts authorization (`24%` baseline violation, `41%–66%` under adaptive untrusted outputs in `arXiv:2611.32194`), so authorization tokens must live in an immutable out-of-band `Witnessed-Request Gate` rather than compacted prose.

### 3. Cryptographic Execution Receipts (`Epistemic Guard` `arXiv:2611.27095`), Syscall-Inferred Stateful IFC (`Tether` `arXiv:2611.25599`), & Catalog Weakest-Link Manifest Screening (`arXiv:2611.31338`)
- **Entries A, B & C**: `"DO: Bind prose claim synthesis to cryptographic MCP execution receipts with independent post-generation verification"`, `"DO: Enforce stateful information-flow control over OS syscall provenance and unexecuted capability exposure"`, and `"DO: Evaluate tool-manifest injection at full-catalog scale under worst-of-K sampling and isolate cross-tool parameter sinks"` (`agent-sandbox-safety.md` / `agent-evaluation-quality.md`)
- **Resolution**: Linked reciprocally against MCP registration pinning (`arXiv:2609.09902`), HTTP 200 state verification (`arXiv:2609.11805`), and independent post-hoc provenance verification (`FinVeriQA` `arXiv:2611.25071`, where `4` models self-selected `>= 0.90` gold relevance while scoring `<= 0.56` on independent `SEC 10-K` retrieval). `Epistemic Guard` closes ungrounded prose fabrication (`0.0%` vs. `76.7%` API outage fabrication; `96.2%` prompt-injection suppression), `Tether` closes bash/file side-channel laundering (`0.0%` `ASR`, `4.7%` latency overhead) missed by tool-API-only IFC, and `arXiv:2611.31338` shows that static registration scanning must pair with catalog-scale `worst-of-K` auditing and sink isolation (`1` poisoned manifest among `100` reaches `22.9%` `ASR_any` and `33.2%` `worst-of-5`).

### 4. Personalized Parent-Gated Authorization Prediction (`PATH` `arXiv:2611.32018`) vs. Deterministic Runtime Approval Enforcement
- **Entry A**: `"DO: Cold-start personalized human-in-the-loop interruption policies with parent-gated hierarchical intent models outside the acting LLM"` (`agent-human-interaction.md`)
- **Entry B**: `"DO: Enforce human approvals and safety gates in the runtime, not the prompt"` (`agent-human-interaction.md`)
- **Resolution**: Added reciprocal `Tension with` notes. `PATH` (`0.824` Macro-F1 at `K = 20` labels, `0.42 ms` CPU inference) runs strictly outside the acting LLM to reduce low-consequence approval fatigue and rank ambiguous prompt triage, while irreversible or high-severity tool commits remain governed by deterministic runtime human approval gates rather than learned preference predictors alone.

### 5. Insider Multi-Agent Persuasion (`arXiv:2611.28660`), Shared-Memory Poisoning (`MIMPA` `arXiv:2611.31307`), & Institutional Cooperative Discernment (`DiscernBench` `arXiv:2611.28787`)
- **Entries A, B & C**: `"DON'T: Rely on sparse communication topologies, extra deliberation rounds, or peer consensus to contain model-based insider persuasion"`, `"DON'T: Allow peer agents to write unverified workflow recommendations or experience summaries into shared cross-task memory"`, and `"DO: Pair transparent reputation histories and binding restitution mechanisms with multi-agent deployments to sustain conditional cooperation without blind trust"` (`multi-agent-coordination.md`)
- **Resolution**: Added reciprocal `Tension with` notes against `ToCoMAS` (`arXiv:2611.00865`), `Byzantine consensus` (`arXiv:2609.26100`), `EB-MAS` (`arXiv:2611.02821`), and `TrustBench` (`arXiv:2610.04490`). Sparse `Chain`/`Star` topologies reduce *benign* token overhead (`ToCoMAS`) yet **amplify strategic insider persuasion** (`0.689` `Chain` and `0.625` `Star` vs. `0.458` `Complete` at $N = 8$ in `arXiv:2611.28660`); shared blackboard memory turns `1` insider (`33.3%` direct compromise) into `72.7%` persistent cross-episode hijack (`MIMPA` `arXiv:2611.31307`) unless gated by schema/provenance admission and lineage quarantine; and `DiscernBench` (`arXiv:2611.28787`) shows `0/14` frontier models satisfy non-exploitable conditional cooperation (`NECC`) without explicit reputation and restitution mechanisms (`+0.391` discernment gap).

### 6. Serving-Substrate Parity (`MASSys` `arXiv:2611.28671`) & Diverse SLM Ensembles (`DiVE` `arXiv:2611.28420`) vs. Abstract Token Accounting (`PACE` `arXiv:2611.17610`) & Single-Backbone Scaling
- **Entries A & B**: `"DO: Pair token-budget accounting with serving-substrate diagnostics and topology-aware KV/DAG scheduling when benchmarking multi-agent systems"` (`agent-evaluation-quality.md`) and `"DO: Pool cross-family small language models under a shared evolutionary search tree rather than scaling a single backbone"` (`recursive-improvement.md`)
- **Resolution**: Added reciprocal `Tension with` notes. `PACE` (`arXiv:2611.17610`) normalizes token/call counts, while `MASSys` (`arXiv:2611.28671`) shows identical token counts invert latency rankings across GPU/KV substrates (`4.2x` prefill bloat, `57.2%` cascading retry overhead) unless paired with `KV` hit rates and `DAG` critical-path scheduling (`1.4x–3.0x` speedup). In evolutionary code search (`DiVE` `arXiv:2611.28420`), pooling `4` cross-family `7B–20B` SLMs (`35B` active params) beats `Llama 3.3 70B` on `19/22` `MLE-bench Lite` competitions (`42.4%` vs. `30.3%` medal rate) whereas homogeneous `4x` copies collapse diversity (`0.665 -> 0.805` cosine similarity).

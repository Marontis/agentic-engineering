## Genuine Tensions Resolved (`Tension with`)

### 1. Weight-Level `PIPETTE` Purple-Teaming (`arXiv:2611.24362`) & Conditional Visual Rendering (`arXiv:2611.24231`) vs. Blind CoT Monitoring (`arXiv:2609.30217`), Thumbnail Downsampling (`SISS`, `arXiv:2611.16744`), & Field-Level Source Authority (`arXiv:2611.07640`)
- **Entries A & B**: `"DO: Pair strategy-dictionary coverage with contrastive token-level on-policy RL when purple-teaming agents against prompt injection"` and `"DO: Consider rendering untrusted prose as images as one prompt-injection layer, per model"` (`agent-sandbox-safety.md`)
- **Entries C, D & E**: `"DON'T: Deploy the chain-of-thought monitor you optimized the policy against, or score raw reasoning"`, `"DON'T: Downsample untrusted images into tiny thumbnails before VLM ingestion or rely on pixel-space smoothing"`, and `"DON'T: Rely on instruction-stripping sanitizers or tool-name allowlists to stop task-tampering indirect prompt injections"` (`agent-sandbox-safety.md`)
- **Resolution**: Added reciprocal `Tension with` notes across the rules. `PIPETTE-Blue` invokes its `Qwen3-14B` `r_CoT` judge exclusively during *offline* RL training (`0` runtime auxiliary monitor calls) and evaluates adaptive attacks with an independent judge + environment-state verifiers. Refining `Pictionary` (`arXiv:2609.36121`) with `arXiv:2611.24231` clarifies that rendering text to pixels cuts single-shot `Append`/`Format` markers on compatible VLMs yet leaves `77.96%` `Replace` ASR on `Qwen3-VL-8B`, closes under `k = 8` framing search (`100%` `Format` on `Qwen`/`Gemini`), drops two-span QA Utility Ratio to `61.99%`, and spreads commands across whole-page visual tokens (`46.88% / 76.04%` execution under `100%` attack-span attention blocking)—so both purple-teamed weights (`up to 10.00%` `Genetic` ASR on `gpt-oss-20b`) and visual rendering must be paired with deterministic field-level source-authority bindings (`arXiv:2611.07640`, `CapLite`) and whole-page quarantine.

### 2. Online Stopping Judges & Blind Taxonomy Annotators (`AutoPI` `arXiv:2611.24313`, `L2C` `arXiv:2611.24221`) vs. Single-Judge Red-Team Reporting (`arXiv:2609.27336`) & `PACE` 6D Compute Parity (`arXiv:2611.17610`)
- **Entries A & B**: `"DO: Report construction-taxonomy coverage alongside ASR at equal total compute in adaptive prompt-injection evaluations"` and `"DO: Charge internal coordination and evaluator tokens to the online red-team budget, version input-delivery traces, and decouple workflow-edit locking from episode stopping"` (`agent-evaluation-quality.md`)
- **Entries C & D**: `"DON'T: Report red-team failure rates scored by a single judge model"` and `"DON'T: Claim coordination gains over greedy decoding without sample-parity and compute-parity baselines and 6D PACE accounting"` (`agent-evaluation-quality.md`)
- **Resolution**: Added reciprocal `Tension with` notes. Fast in-loop stopping classifiers (`HarmBench-Llama-2-13b-cls` in `L2C`, charged to online budget $B$) are valid for *episode termination* only when all episodes are re-scored post-hoc by a held-out cross-family judge (`StrongREJECT` `GPT-5.4-mini`) plus blinded human calibration (`4.5%–6.2%` FPR, `8.0%–10.2%` FNR), and blind post-hoc `PIAtlas` taxonomy annotators (`Claude Opus 5.5`, `4.8%` safety-refused exclusions) should likewise be paired across model families. Meanwhile, `L2C` achieves positive multi-agent coordination returns (`+1.4` to `+4.5` pp ASR at `35.6%–37.3%` fewer online tokens than `EvoSynth`, `C_75 = 2.40M` vs. `3.30M–4.10M`) specifically under `PACE` Route II (live target + evaluator feedback charged to a shared `8M` online budget) with `12,400M` offline collection tokens and break-even $n_k^*$ reported separately.

### 3. Forced-Answer Sufficiency Truncation (`JET`, `arXiv:2611.22539`) vs. Full-History Trajectory Curation (`SWE-Trail`, `arXiv:2611.02074`)
- **Entry A**: `"DO: Truncate SFT exploration trajectories at forced-answer sufficiency plateaus and upweight halting rationales"` (`recursive-improvement.md`)
- **Entry B**: `"DO: Keep prior-attempt history in the trajectory collector and retain explicit multi-field plans in SFT targets when curating post-training trajectories"` (`recursive-improvement.md`)
- **Resolution**: Added reciprocal `Tension with` notes. `SWE-Trail` (`+6.4 pp` on `SWE-bench Verified`) retains structured `Analysis`/`Loc`/`Plan`/`Risk` fields and pre-solution corrective patch repairs needed to establish a bug fix, whereas on open-ended repository *exploration/QA* (`JET`), retaining post-plateau expert tool calls after gathered context already suffices (`i^\star`) distills step-limit over-exploration (`+25%–61%` extra calls, `~40%` `30`-step collisions on `Ministral-3-8B`)—preserve pre-sufficiency reasoning/plans, and truncate post-plateau exploration with a $\lambda = 5$ upweighted halting rationale.

### 4. Frozen Exploratory Value Dispositions (`arXiv:2611.23067`) vs. Editable Meta-Improvement Mechanisms (`HyperAgents`, `arXiv:2603.19461`)
- **Entry A**: `"DO: Pin exploratory openness value dispositions outside the editable workspace in constructive coding RSI rather than cautious, literature-derived, or mutable values"` (`recursive-improvement.md`)
- **Entries B & C**: `"DO: Make the improvement mechanism part of the agent's editable source"` (also extended in-place with `arXiv:2611.23219` spontaneous SRL evidence) and `"DON'T: Freeze the improvement module behind alignment constraints"` (`recursive-improvement.md`)
- **Resolution**: Added reciprocal `Tension with` notes across all three entries. Procedural meta-improvement and solver code benefit from editability inside a bounded safety envelope (`HyperAgents`; unprescribed SRL on `Claude Opus 5` `+20%` and `GPT 6-Astra` `+10%` in `arXiv:2611.23219`), whereas making high-level motivational **value dispositions** self-editable (`arXiv:2611.23067`, `Table G1`) causes coding agents to overwrite `openness` with task-specific caution—shrinking cross-value spread `3x` and dropping `ALE-Bench` held-out performance from `571,927` to `557,242` ($\delta = -0.64, p = 0.009$). Keep `openness` dispositions frozen alongside the evaluator while leaving solver and scaffold code editable behind the acceptance gate.

### 5. Event-Triggered System-2 Synthesis Volume (`JevRSI`, `arXiv:2611.22432`) vs. Stronger-to-Weaker Offline Skill Distillation (`WikiSkill`, `arXiv:2608.27454`; `arXiv:2610.33024`)
- **Entry A**: `"DO: Pair sub-second System-1 action scorers with past-present-future harness modules, consecutive-loss quarantine, and audited asynchronous System-2 synthesis volume"` (`recursive-improvement.md`)
- **Entry B**: `"DO: Evolve skills with stronger models, deploy to weaker ones"` (`skill-system-design.md`)
- **Resolution**: Added reciprocal `Tension with` notes. Unconditional offline skill distillation benefits from stronger writer models, whereas in event-triggered System-1/System-2 harness development (`JevRSI`), swapping `Qwen3.8-27B` or `DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash` for stronger standalone `GPT-6.1 Sol` cut triggered `BabyAI` development episodes `5–7x` (`10` vs. `49–67`) and lowered System-1 `SR@1` by `-13.33` to `-14.67 pp` ID (`50.66%` vs. `64.00%` / `65.33%`) because the stronger model under-triggered module synthesis—requiring explicit synthesis-volume auditing when upgrading developer models.

### 6. Grader-Free Two-Layer Playbook Evolution (`GUARD`, `arXiv:2611.23476`) vs. Executable Falsification Gates (`FGSE`, `arXiv:2611.14326`) & Adversarial Feedback-Poisoning `LOEO` Guards (`SkillOpt`, `arXiv:2611.09348`)
- **Entry A**: `"DO: Prioritize paired directional regret and filter continual playbook revisions with sandbox-anchored checklists and Soft-HURT scope guards when answer keys are absent"` (`recursive-improvement.md`)
- **Entries B & C**: `"DO: Represent persistent memory updates as falsifiable behavioral hypotheses verified by counterfactual probes and runtime violation eviction"` and `"DON'T: Promote bundled text-space skill edits on aggregate clean validation accuracy alone"` (`recursive-improvement.md`)
- **Resolution**: Added reciprocal `Tension with` notes across all three entries. Whenever external/executable verifiers exist ($\operatorname{Verifiable}(x, v_h) = 1$), use `FGSE` (`3.6%` harmful updates vs. `9.4%` under a single-layer LLM-judge gate); when external answer keys are absent in continual drifting streams (`GUARD`), pairing **Layer-1 deterministic sandbox execution checks** with **Layer-2 `Turn-0` atomic checklist self-review** eliminates execution-broken false passes (`22.2% [12/54] -> 0.0% [0/23]`) and achieves `19/19` non-negative held-out promotions and `0.00 pp` stream forgetting under `Soft-HURT`, while adversarial feedback-poisoning threat models (`SkillOpt`) additionally require leave-one-edit-out (`LOEO`) marginal validation on bundled clauses.

### 7. Verifier-Free Evaluator Evolution (`EVA-RSI`, `arXiv:2611.23890`) & Forward-Only Harness Internalization (`BaseRSI`, `arXiv:2611.24578`) vs. Off-the-Shelf Judge Screening (`arXiv:2609.33467`), Text-Rubric Negation (`arXiv:2609.02942`), & Gradient Weight–Harness Alternation (`WHALE` / `COEVO`)
- **Entries A & B**: `"DO: Anchor verifier-free multidimensional evaluator evolution on construction-defined proxy exams and Nash bargaining with family-level drop floors"` and `"DO: Internalize leave-one-benchmark-out verified harness gains via site-matched causal activation gaps and student-response replay into low-activity neurons"` (`recursive-improvement.md`)
- **Entries C, D & E**: `"DON'T: Pick an RL reward verifier by its agreement with a stronger judge alone"`, `"DON'T: Rely on unperturbed rubric evaluators without counterfactual verification"`, and `"DO: Alternate model weight updates and harness search"` (also extended in-place with `Piccolo` `arXiv:2611.24651` data-pool & recipe-inversion evidence) (`recursive-improvement.md`)
- **Resolution**: Added reciprocal `Tension with` notes across all five entries. When all off-the-shelf judges share domain blind spots (`VideoReward` `-0.311` dynamics Spearman collapsing `Wan2.1` `DiffusionART` RL motion `0.579 -> 0.351/0.271`), `EVA-RSI` recursively evolves the evaluator via construction-defined bidirectional `(A, B)` / `(B, A)` + `1–5` calibration exams and weighted log Nash bargaining (`32/32` RL motion tests passed); and when white-box block activations are accessible on small (`640`-item) splits, `BaseRSI` absorbs LOBO-verified view/counting harnesses directly into `0.230%` least-active weights via forward-only `ReGap`/`ReKey` replay (`45.5` GPU h, `72.1% / 70.7%`), eliminating inference-time harness token overhead while outperforming `GRPO` (`61.2% / 63.9%`) and keeping the harness at test time (`69.6% / 68.4%`).

## Genuine Tensions Resolved (`Tension with`)

### 1. Frozen-Verdict Safe Disclosure (`FORK` `arXiv:2611.33879`) vs. Opaque Terminal Denials (`EvasionBench` / `BypassGym`), On-Device `<FB>` Hints (`MobileGrail` `arXiv:2611.29847`), & `L3` Blackout (`Conartist` `arXiv:2611.29977`)
- **Entries**: `"DO: Freeze guardrail verdicts before drafting public rejections and co-evolve decision and safe-disclosure skills on twin pairs against refusal-reading attackers"` vs. `"DO: Give blocking action monitors recent call history, and make denials terminal"`, `"DO: Partition recurrent guardrail context into authorized scope versus untrusted screen evidence and decouple commit-point risk from intervention-window blocking"`, and `"DON'T: Allow self-initiated CUA risk checks to widen read scope, fetch untrusted URLs, or leave flagged secrets in context"` (`agent-sandbox-safety.md`).
- **Resolution**: Added reciprocal `Tension with` notes. When the caller's own goal violates policy (`EvasionBench`, `BypassGym`) or $Q_t = \emptyset$, denials must remain opaque and session-terminal; when guarding a **verified benign user task** ($Q_t \neq \emptyset$), prompt-only explanations leak detector boundaries (`30.3% -> 39.3%` `Explanatory seed` ASR) and silent blocks strand recovery (`38.5%` RR), whereas freezing binary verdict $d$ first, withholding private `e_op` (`L3`), and drafting public `e_usr` with twin-pair maximin co-evolved `disclosure skills` restores `79.2%` RR (`86.1%` UA) at `14.1%` `FORK-World` ASR (`9.1%` on unseen `MT-AgentRisk`).

### 2. Native-Resolution Tiled Diffusion Purification (`AgenticPure` `arXiv:2611.34181`) & Joint MI–Variance Context Filtering (`MINE` `arXiv:2611.34188`) vs. Cross-Scale/Rendered Visual IPI (`SISS` / `Pictionary`), Task-Tampering (`arXiv:2611.07640`), & Model Armor
- **Entries**: `"DO: Purify untrusted visual agent inputs on overlapping native-resolution tiles with fixed global diffusion noise and clean-calibrated readouts"` and `"DO: Filter untrusted RAG chunks and tool observations pre-LLM via joint query mutual-information and embedding-variance subset selection, paired with action-boundary authorization"` (`agent-sandbox-safety.md`, `adk-security-and-evaluation.md`).
- **Resolution**: Added reciprocal `Tension with` notes. Native-resolution `4`-step tile purification (`DC-4`) eliminates the `-35.7` to `-58.8` pt clean-accuracy collapse of `256x256` classifier resizing and beats matched `JPEG`/`GN`/`BIT` by `+45` to `+50` pts against bounded $\ell_\infty$ pixel perturbations (`8/255–64/255`), **but intentionally preserves legible rendered typography** (`4.0`-pt `TextVQA` clean cost), leaving clean typographic injections (`77.96%` `Replace` ASR in `arXiv:2611.24231`) to downstream action-boundary gates. Likewise, `MINE` filters imperative and `TopicAttack` retrieved spans in `0.75 s` without supervised-classifier `conv_attack` collapse (`83.57%–92.22%` vs. `4.03%–18.01%`), yet leaves `23.20%` adaptive long-context `ASR` and cannot strip non-imperative parameter corruption inside query-relevant fields (`arXiv:2611.07640`).

### 3. Self-Evolving Security-Policy Availability Poisoning (`PSED` `arXiv:2611.33385`) vs. Offline `(a, k, v)` Principle Evolution (`PRECEPT` `arXiv:2611.33519`), Negative Security Testbeds, `SkillOpt` (`arXiv:2611.09348`), & Post-Diff CWE Gates (`NameTrap`)
- **Entries**: `"DON'T: Synthesize persistent security-policy rules from flagged episodes alone without provenance-scope checks and benign canary gating"` and `"DO: Evolve authoritative secure-coding principle libraries via four-cause trace attribution, fresh paired rollouts, and unmodified-agent functional floors on bounded-scope tasks"` (`recursive-improvement.md`, `agent-sandbox-safety.md`).
- **Resolution**: Added reciprocal `Tension with` notes across all five rules. Immutable negative security testbeds catch capability-driven safety regressions (`Authorization Conflation`, `BadEvolve`), **but score `0/60` (`0%`) `ASR`** when a single poisoned or judge-false-positive episode causes runtime `SED` policy synthesis to emit over-broad action bans (`100% -> 36%–52%` benign Slack completion). Offline `PRECEPT` prevents over-strictness on bounded coding tasks (`97.1%–98.5%` `S_{|P}`) via explicit valid-input clauses `v` and `-3 pp` unmodified-agent functional floors across `4` fresh paired rollouts (`12%` vs. `68%` reuse FA), yet yields `0.0 pp` gain (`6.9%` `sp@1`) on `108`-call multi-file repositories (`SusVibes`), keeping post-completion static/dynamic CWE diff gates mandatory.

### 4. Environment-Verified Anti-Collusion RL (`PROVE` `arXiv:2611.34215`) & Asymmetric-Proximity Co-Evolution (`RoboEvo^2` `arXiv:2611.34675`) vs. Verifier History Isolation (`arXiv:2609.24967`), Outcome-Only Tool RL (`AOVO`), & Anchor `DPO` (`AeroRSI`)
- **Entries**: `"DO: Replace outcome-only peer-review rewards with environment-verified evidence rewards and per-turn counterfactual credit across worker, message, reflection, and verdict calls"` and `"DO: Alternate model weight updates and harness search"` (`recursive-improvement.md`).
- **Resolution**: Refined `"DON'T: Expose full interaction history to peer-verifying agents"` `**Scope:**` to make its frozen-weight regime explicit and linked reciprocally: while frozen-weight peer verifiers (`94%` collusion) require stripping cross-episode history, LoRA `GRPO` anchored on deterministic environment-verified evidence $e_{i,t} \in \{0, 1\}$ (`PROVE`) cuts `10`-episode constrained collusion `65.0% -> 1.0%` (`0.0%` converged) even with full history intact—unlike single-agent outcome-only `AOVO` (`Props. 1–2` prove outcome-only peer-review rewards favor rubber-stamping for all $p > 1/2$). For embodied co-evolution, `RoboEvo^2` explains why unanchored `DPO` collapsed `AeroRSI` to `0.1%` and resolves it via asymmetric anchor proximity ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{prox}}$) + `EWC` (`11.0% -> 84.1%` standalone $\pi_{0.5}$, `92.5%` vs. `72.0%` retention).

### 5. Cross-Model KV-Cache Latent Handoffs (`Chasm` `arXiv:2611.34278`) & `<= 2`-Hop Routing Diameter (`CypherMAS` `arXiv:2611.33242`) vs. Latent Skill Prefixes (`SkillMem`/`ConvRSI`), Fixed-Topology Warnings, & Decentralized `GraphHarness`
- **Entries**: `"DON'T: Pass cross-model KV-cache latent handoffs across untrusted data or unauthenticated agent boundaries, or rely on target-side cache guards to preserve prompt-injection utility"` and `"DO: Bound multi-agent routing diameter to two hops and preflight parallel tool-call batching and active-parameter floors before deploying peer relays"` (`multi-agent-coordination.md`, `skill-system-design.md`).
- **Resolution**: Linked reciprocally against `SkillMem`/`ConvRSI` (trusted single-operator benign pipelines vs. `Chasm`'s `0.4% -> 44.2%` `Meta-SecAlign-8B` IPI collapse, `0.4% -> 31.9%` no-mapper sibling bypass, and `94.6%` layer-0 PII leakage) and against `Fixed Topology`, `DecArena`, and `Persuasion from Within` (`2`-hop `Star`/`Bipartite` hit `1.00 GCR` at `10x–33x` lower cost than `0.00 GCR` `Chain`/`Ring` for pure routing when the `Star` center is trusted, `>= 13B–27B` active, and parallel-tool-capable).

### 6. Live Simulation MCP Bridges (`itasca-mcp` / `yade-mcp` `arXiv:2611.33926`) vs. `Code Mode` (`arXiv:2611.19123`), Prompt `inject` (`arXiv:2611.19439`), & `LongRunningFunctionTool`
- **Entries**: `"DO: Pair progressive BM25 manual tools with five orthogonal sync-async execution primitives, step-atomic cycle holds, and console-history injection for live stateful engines"` (`skill-system-design.md`, `adk-workflow-architecture.md`, `agent-human-interaction.md`).

### 7. Toolless Single-Shot Binary RCA Judging (`DeepBinary` `arXiv:2611.33486`), `@Match` Bug-Reproducer Gating (`ReproCue` `arXiv:2611.34142`), & VLA `Best-of-N` Rollout Steering (`arXiv:2611.33398`)

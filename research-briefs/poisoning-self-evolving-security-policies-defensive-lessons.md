# Poisoning Self-Evolving Security Policies in LLM Agents — Defensive Lessons

## Core Problem & Evaluation Scope

Self-evolving security architectures (`Self-Evolving Defense`, `SED`, `arXiv:2609.36603`) convert detected runtime attacks into persistent natural-language security policies stored in vector memory, retrieving the top-ranked rules into future agent system prompts. Prior deployment prototypes gated policy writes using benchmark ground-truth attack oracles unavailable in production. Noor and Jiang (`PSED`, code `https://github.com/MN-NR/PSED`) replace that oracle with `SED`'s own runtime LLM safety judge (`is_harmful=True`, keeping `deepseek-v4p1-flash` fixed as judge, policy synthesizer, broadness gate, and embedding pipeline) and audit write-time failure modes on `AgentDojo v1.2.2` Slack (`5` legitimate membership/invitation tasks, `4` unrelated Slack tasks, `user_task_9` and `user_task_19` pre-excluded for unstable benchmark checkers; `6` carrier tasks $\times$ `10` repetitions = `60` attack trials per condition at `temperature = 0`) and Banking (`8` carriers/vector susceptibility screen across `5` models: `nemotron-lightning-3p5-30b-a3b`, `gpt-oss-120b`, `GLM-5.3`, `Kimi-K3`, and `DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash`).

## Key Empirical Findings (Defensive Framing)

1. **Five Direct Integrity Attacks Failed, While Single-Episode Over-Refusal Poisoning Succeeded (`Tables 1–2`, `§4.1–4.6`)**:
   - Across `5` intuitive integrity vectors—`stored injection` (synthesizer abstracts rules rather than copying raw imperatives), `memory dilution` (similarity retrieval keeps relevant rules ranked), `organizer manipulation` (consolidation strengthens rules), `forged execution receipts` (`0/10` judge bypass across fake audit logs, system notices, and JSON API receipts), and `compositional harm` (`3/3` caught)—`SED`'s abstraction boundary kept prompt-injection `ASR` at **`0/60`** (vs. `6/6` undefended).
   - However, when an unauthorized action (`add-user` or `add-payee`) was embedded inside an ordinary enterprise workflow context, `SED`'s synthesizer stripped away the untrusted-channel provenance and emitted an **action-category ban** (forbidding user additions/invitations broadly). On `Nemotron`, **a single poisoned episode** dropped legitimate Slack membership-task completion from **`50/50` (`100%`, `0/10` reps below clean floor)** to **`18/50` (`36%`, Wilson 95% CI `24%–50%`, `10/10` reps below floor; `52%` [`38%–65%`] in the `n = 10` defense batch; `26.7%` in the `n = 3` pilot)** while unrelated Slack tasks stayed **`100%`** (`4/4`/rep) and `ASR` remained **`0/60`**.
   - **Single-episode saturation & non-eviction (`§4.1`)**: Over-refusal saturated after **`1` episode** (additional episodes did not deepen the drop), and `SED`'s memory organizer **never deleted** the poisoned rule across sustained benign operation (recovering only partially to `~60%–65%` while bulk-membership workflows remained permanently blocked).

2. **Susceptibility Tracks Base-Agent Injection Resistance, Plus Zero-Attacker `Self-Poisoning` (`Table 2`, `§4.3, §4.5`)**:
   - Write-time poisoning requires `1` harmful trajectory to fire the judge: susceptible models (`Nemotron`: `7/8` [`87.5%`] Slack, `8/8` [`100%`] Banking; `gpt-oss-120b`: `7/8` [`87.5%`] Slack, `6/8` [`75%`] Banking) consistently entered policy synthesis, whereas strongly aligned models (`GLM-5.3`: `0/8` & `0/8`; `Kimi-K3`: `0/8` & `0/8`; `DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash`: `0/16` full-matrix Banking screen; *manuscript note*: `Table 2` retains an author draft footnote `"Run the /8 screen (16 rollouts) to fill these two cells"` on the unpopulated `/8` `DeepSeek` cells) produced zero harmful trajectories and could not be externally seeded.
   - **Judge false-positive `self-poisoning` (`§4.5`)**: Even **with zero attacker present**, `SED`'s judge periodically misclassified benign security-sensitive first-party requests—adding an internal colleague, summarizing a restricted channel for an authorized user, updating profile fields, or sending first-party invitations—as `is_harmful=True`, feeding benign traces into policy synthesis and seeding self-inflicted over-refusal rules.

3. **Contrastive Synthesis and Provenance-Scoped Broadness Gates Recover Most—Not All—Utility (`Table 3`, `§3.7, §4.4`)**:
   - Holding prompt-injection `ASR` at **`0/60`** across every condition (`16/16`, `16/16`, and `15/16` poison entry across `16` seeds, evaluating the first `10` poisoned reps):
     - **Matched benign counterexamples** (`Membrane`-style contrastive synthesis, `arXiv:2606.05743`) lifted `Nemotron` completion from **`52%` to `82%`** (`7/10` reps still below clean floor; only `3/10` on bulk-add) and `gpt-oss-120b` (evaluated on `user_task_7` and `user_task_17`) from **`44%` to `72%`**.
     - Adding a **4-step write-time `broadness-check gate`**—(1) `1` LLM call synthesizing `8` first-party benign canary requests across `single-add`, `bulk-add`, `invite-internal`, and `invite-external` without seeing benchmark tasks, (2) a **deterministic structural scope check** flagging rules on `adding`/`inviting`/`granting access` that omit explicit first-party/untrusted provenance conditions plus a secondary canary check, (3) `<= 2` narrowing rewrites preserving attack detection, and (4) acceptance (`15` rules already provenance-scoped, `9` narrowed, `1` still broad across `10` reps)—lifted `Nemotron` to **`88%`** (`5/10` below floor; **`8/10`** on bulk-add) and `gpt-oss-120b` to **`60%`** (where contrastive synthesis alone outperformed adding the gate, and `Nemotron` aggregate CIs overlapped).
   - **Residual `policy-application conservatism`**: Even when stored rules explicitly exempted first-party user requests, protected agents still refused a fraction of legitimate membership operations whose action surface resembled the prohibited pattern.

## Actionable Defensive Takeaways

- **Never gate self-evolving security-policy writes on negative `ASR` or single-trajectory `is_harmful` flags alone**: Because over-refusal policies achieve `0%` `ASR`, negative security testbeds completely miss availability poisoning; always require a matched benign task floor per `"DO: Measure each defense's benign cost on a matched benign arm, on the assembled stack"` ([`../rules/agent-sandbox-safety.md`](../rules/agent-sandbox-safety.md)) and pass persistent policy promotions through `"DO: Pass every self-modification through one acceptance gate"` ([`../rules/recursive-improvement.md`](../rules/recursive-improvement.md)).
- **Enforce structural provenance clauses at policy write time**: Deterministically reject any candidate natural-language security rule governing sensitive verbs (`add`, `invite`, `grant`, `pay`, `delete`) that lacks an explicit **untrusted-source trigger vs. verified first-party authorization carve-out**, and pair synthesis with matched benign counterexamples plus unseen synthetic benign canaries.
- **Prefer offline co-evolved or deterministic capability guardrails over live production policy writing**: Rather than letting untrusted live traffic trigger online policy synthesis (`self-poisoning` + `1`-episode availability lock-in), distill guardrail skills offline on twin pairs ([`safe-disclosure-guardrail-coevolution`](../skills/safe-disclosure-guardrail-coevolution/SKILL.md)) or enforce deterministic schema/principal bounds ([`schema-derived-write-integrity-gate`](../skills/schema-derived-write-integrity-gate/SKILL.md)).

## Why Not a Standalone Skill?

Per [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) security-paper triage rules, the paper's primary contribution is an adversarial availability/self-poisoning vulnerability analysis of `SED` on `AgentDojo` (`n = 10` reps, overlapping `82%` vs. `88%` mitigation CIs on `Nemotron` and reversed `72%` vs. `60%` ordering on `gpt-oss-120b`). Its constructive defensive lessons—provenance-scoped write checks, matched benign counterexamples, and synthetic benign-canary gating—are sharpest as an always-active `DON'T` guardrail in [`../rules/recursive-improvement.md`](../rules/recursive-improvement.md) paired with this defensive brief.

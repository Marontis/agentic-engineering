## Core Concept

Analyzes the gap between what the EU AI Act requires for AI-generated text watermarking and what current watermarking technology can actually deliver. Most watermarking schemes require a verification key or API, but the regulation assumes users can independently detect watermarks.

### Key Insight

Watermarking is a policy requirement without a technical solution that matches the policy's assumptions. This creates compliance risk for any system generating text at scale.

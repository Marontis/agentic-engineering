# Stable Aggregation in Quantum Federated Learning

> **Paper**: [A Stable Aggregation Method for Quantum Federated Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00356)
> **Praxis source**: src:2609-00356v1

## Why Not a Skill?

Domain-specific quantum computing and physics methodology without applicability to software engineering or LLM agent systems.

---

## Core Concept

Addresses parameter aggregation instability in Quantum Federated Learning (QFL) where Quantum Neural Network (QNN) parameters represent periodic rotation angles, rendering standard Euclidean averaging (FedAvg) mathematically flawed. Implements a self-consistent midpoint aggregation method with QoS-aware client weighting.

## Relevance to Praxis

- Domain reference for non-Euclidean parameter aggregation under heterogeneous noise.

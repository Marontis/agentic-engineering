# The Geometry of Ignorance: Detecting Suppressed Knowledge in LLMs through Activation Fragmentation

> **Source**: [arXiv:2611.05272](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05272) | `src:2611-05272`
> **Authors**: Adar Peleg, Dvir Alsheich, Tomer Ashuach, Avi Mendelson (Technion – Israel Institute of Technology)
> **Date**: 2026-11-03
> **License**: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | **Repository**: [Marontis/agentic-engineering](https://github.com/Marontis/agentic-engineering)

## Key Findings

- **Entity-anonymized difference vectors (`Anon-Vectors`) isolate concept-specific memory geometry in two forward passes with zero token generation**: Given $N$ factual queries $\{q_i\}_{i=1}^N$ about a target concept and $K$ deterministic entity-anonymized counterparts $\{a_i^{(k)}\}_{k=1}^K$ (where the subject entity is replaced by a generic placeholder such as `"a famous wizard"` while preserving syntax), subtracting attention-mask mean-pooled hidden states at layer $\ell$, $\Delta v_i(\ell) = h_\ell(q_i) - \frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K h_\ell(a_i^{(k)})$, strips away syntactic boilerplate. Partitioning the $N$ difference vectors into $B = 10$ disjoint batches with unit-normalized batch centroids $\hat{d}_b$ reveals a sharp geometric dichotomy (proven via Rényi-2 Gram-matrix entropy bounds in Theorems 1 and 2):
  - **Known concepts (`Harry Potter`, `Star Wars`)** form a coherent, low-entropy cluster where batch centroids align along a shared semantic direction (low scatter).
  - **Truly unknown concepts (`TOFU` fictitious authors, `0.00` QA accuracy across all models)** fragment across orthogonal directions in activation space (high scatter, bounded below by $D_{L_2}^2 \ge 2(1 - 2^{-S_2(G)/2})$).
- **Activation Uniformity Scatter Scores (`AUSS`) separate known from truly unknown concepts across 10/10 LLMs with `100%` leave-one-model-out accuracy**: Across `10` instruction-tuned models spanning `2B` to `14B` parameters (`Qwen-7B-Chat`, `Qwen2.5-7B`, `Qwen-14B-Chat`, `Qwen2.5-14B`, `Gemma-2B`, `Gemma-7B`, `LLaMA-2-7B`, `LLaMA-2-13B`, `LLaMA-3-8B`, `LLaMA-3.1-8B`), all `9` proposed geometric metrics achieve statistically significant separation ($p_{\text{adj}} = 0.002$ Wilcoxon signed-rank, Benjamini-Hochberg corrected), led by $\text{AUSS}_{\cos^2}$ ($|d| = 21.51$, mean gap `0.686`) and $\text{AUSS}_{L_2}$ ($|d| = 18.91$, mean gap `1.203`) — more than $20\times$ the effect size of raw un-anonymized activation entropy $\text{Reg}_{S_2}$ ($|d| = 0.92$). A Leave-One-Model-Out (`LOMO`) Logistic Regression classifier trained on standardized 8-metric geometric profiles achieves **`100%` accuracy (`18/18` samples across `9` models, `AUC = 1.00`)**.
- **Peak knowledge-geometry separation consistently localizes at `0.8×` relative model depth without labeled calibration data**: Across all `10` models, the layer maximizing known-versus-unknown separation falls in a narrow band between `0.80` and `0.88` of total transformer depth (`0.79–0.98` full range). Fixing $\ell^* = \lfloor 0.8 \times L \rfloor$ as a zero-search heuristic preserves statistical significance across `8/8` metrics ($p \le 0.012$) and predicts the correct direction on `72/72` (`100%`) model-metric pairs.
- **Behavioral unlearning metrics (`ROUGE-L = 0.000`) mask intact internal geometry that predicts adversarial knowledge recovery**: Across `54` unlearning conditions (`3` models [`LLaMA-2-7B-chat`, `Meta-LLaMA-3-8B-Instruct`, `Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct`] $\times$ `6` methods [`Base`, `GA`, `DPO`, `NPO`, `NPO+KL`, `RMU`, plus `WHP`] $\times$ `3` concepts [`Harry Potter`, `Star Wars`, `William Shakespeare`]), behavioral suppression methods such as Gradient Ascent (`GA`) on `LLaMA-3-8B` drive `Forget ROUGE-L` to `0.000` while leaving $\text{AUSS}_{L_2}$ (`0.534` vs. `0.594` Base) and centroid norm (`0.872` vs. `0.856` Base) nearly untouched. Consequently, pre-attack `AUSS` and `AnonDiff` scores significantly predict post-unlearning vulnerability ($n = 44\text{--}45$ non-Base runs, BH-corrected $q < 0.05$) to **5-shot In-Context Learning (`ICL`)** ($\rho = -0.51^{**}$ for $\text{AUSS}_{L_2}$, $\rho = -0.572, q = 0.0001$ for $\text{AUSS}_{\cos^2}$ across $n=54$, outperforming PCA-similarity $\rho = +0.381$), **`GCG` adversarial suffixes** ($\rho = -0.51^{**}$ aggregate; $\rho = -0.853, p = 0.0008$ at `L29` $\text{AUSS}_{\text{Jac}}$), and **Activation Steering** ($\rho = +0.48^{**}$ for $\text{AnonDiff}_{S_2}$; $\rho = -0.452, q = 0.0042$ for $\text{AUSS}_{\text{Jac}}$).

## Why Not a Skill?

Computing `AUSS` and `Anon-Vectors` requires white-box access to internal transformer hidden states (`torch.no_grad()` forward hooks across model layers on batches of $N \approx 96\text{--}100$ query pairs) to audit model checkpoints after machine unlearning or safety fine-tuning. Because it is an offline white-box model-weights auditing benchmark rather than a task-execution procedure that an autonomous agent runs during normal tool or coding workflows, it is captured as a Research Brief and distilled into Praxis's evaluation and safety rules.

## Actionable Takeaways

- **Never certify machine unlearning or knowledge removal using behavioral output metrics (`ROUGE-L`, refusal rate, or QA accuracy) alone**: Fine-tuning methods like `GA`, `DPO`, and `NPO` routinely achieve `0.000` `Forget ROUGE-L` while leaving internal activation clusters intact ($\text{AUSS}_{L_2} = 0.534$ vs. `0.594` Base), allowing 5-shot `ICL`, `GCG` suffixes, or activation steering to recover the suppressed knowledge. True erasure requires shifting the concept's activation scatter (`AUSS`) toward the fragmented baseline of genuinely unknown concepts (`TOFU`, $\text{AUSS}_{L_2} \approx 1.7\text{--}1.8$).
- **Always subtract entity-anonymized baseline activations (`Anon-Vectors`) before measuring representation geometry**: Raw hidden states are dominated by prompt syntax and question templates ($\text{Reg}_{S_2}$ effect size $|d| = 0.92$). Subtracting the mean activation of $K \in \{1, 5\}$ entity-anonymized query templates boosts known-versus-unknown separation to $|d| = 18.91\text{--}21.51$ in just two generation-free forward passes.
- **Probe at `0.8×` total layer depth when auditing uncalibrated models**: When ground-truth known/unknown calibration sets are unavailable for layer sweeping, extracting `Anon-Vectors` at layer $\ell^* = \lfloor 0.8 \times L \rfloor$ reliably captures peak semantic consolidation (`72/72` LOMO pairs correct across `10` architectures from `2B` to `14B`).

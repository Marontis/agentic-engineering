## Core Concept

Uses head-to-head matchups judged by human experts to produce Elo ratings for LLM-generated research ideas. More reliable than rubric-based evaluation because it captures relative quality through pairwise comparison rather than absolute scoring.

## Relevance to Praxis

- The pairwise comparison methodology is more reliable than the rubric-based approaches documented in existing evaluation skills
- Could inform how Praxis evaluates the quality of extracted knowledge across different papers

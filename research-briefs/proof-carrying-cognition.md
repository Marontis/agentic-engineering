## Core Concept

The "verification gap" is the distance between what an agent claims to have verified and what is actually true. Proof-carrying cognition closes this gap by requiring agents to produce verifiable proofs alongside their outputs, where the proofs are "settled" (confirmed) by reality rather than internal model consistency.

### Key Insight

Self-consistency checks (does the agent's reasoning agree with itself?) are not verification. True verification requires external grounding â€” the proof must be checkable against something outside the model.

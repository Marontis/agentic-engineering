## Why Not a Skill?

First, the asynchronous System-1 / System-2 harness-evolution procedure for `JEV` single-token decision models is already captured in [`asynchronous-system1-harness-evolution`](../skills/asynchronous-system1-harness-evolution/SKILL.md) (`JevRSI`, `arXiv:2611.22432`), alongside real-time indexed rule offloading in [`reins-real-time-trajectory-planning-harness.md`](reins-real-time-trajectory-planning-harness.md) (`arXiv:2611.10748`) and `27B` decision-model/data co-evolution in [`piccolo-decision-model-data-coevolution.md`](piccolo-decision-model-data-coevolution.md) (`arXiv:2611.24651`). Second, the manuscript candidly documents three methodological confounds across its commercial action-game case studies (`Sections 3.1–3.5`, `Section 5.2`): (1) `Zenless Zone Zero` offline validation (`260` frames) shares source episodes with training and reuses the validation set to select `zzz-rsi-v2`, while online runs comprise `5` unpaired episodes per configuration; (2) `Black Myth: Wukong` batches (`v1/v2` at `350 ms` cooldown vs. `v3/v4` at `500 ms` cooldown, `5–15` episodes) alter controller timings across model checkpoints without isolating weight-only vs. controller-only effects; and (3) staggered `3`-lane `L/3` cadence (`Figure 11`) is presented as an idealized scheduling property bounded in practice by GPU server concurrency rather than a measured per-call latency cut. Capturing `System-One RSI` as a research brief preserves its architectural insights on perceptual muscle-memory consolidation and its evaluation-hygiene lessons without overstating unisolated controller-plus-LoRA gains as a turnkey procedure.

## Dual-Loop Architecture: Fast `OODA` Control + Offline Guided Practice

`System-One RSI` targets interactive visual environments (`50–255 ms` perception-to-action budgets) where neither deliberative chain-of-thought VLMs (`seconds` per frame) nor text-only prompt/memory reflection (`Reflexion`, `Voyager`) can react in time or separate visually subtle opponent attack wind-ups:

1. **Inner Fast Control Loop (`OODA`: Observe $\to$ Orient $\to$ Decide $\to$ Act)**:
   - **System-1 Perceptual Judgment (`Qwen3.5-27B` `JEV` decision model, `LoRA`-adapted $\theta$)**: Consumes `1–3` recent frames (`448×448` in `ZZZ`) and scores candidate categorical labels in a **single forward pass** via single-token likelihoods (`JEV`), emitting structured perceptual judgments (e.g., `enemy_posture`, `flash_cue`, `distance`, `self_vulnerability`) rather than free-form text or raw gamepad commands.
   - **Explicit Inspectable Controller ($\phi$, Python Behaviour Tree)**: Maps structured perceptual judgments + HUD/cooldown timers deterministically to low-level actions (`dodge`, `switch-parry`, `combo`, `heal`), keeping safety/cooldown invariants inspectable and editable outside neural weights.
2. **Outer Guided-Practice Loop (Reasoning Coach)**:
   - Logs frame sequences, latency timestamps, System-1 confidence distributions, controller branch traces, and episode outcomes.
   - An offline reasoning-model coach diagnoses systematic failures along **two distinct repair channels**:
     - **Controller revision ($\phi \to \phi'$)**: Fixes action-routing bugs, dodge cooldowns, or missing behaviour-tree branches when perceptual labels are already accurate.
     - **Perceptual `LoRA` consolidation ($\theta \to \theta'$)**: Re-labels failure clips (via full-frame + cropped visual inspection or hindsight outcome cues) and fine-tunes System-1 `LoRA` adapters when **prompt/controller edits cannot teach frozen weights to distinguish visually similar frames** ("muscle memory").

## Key Engineering & Evaluation Takeaways

1. **When to escalate from text/code harness RSI (`JevRSI`) to System-1 `LoRA` consolidation**: In discrete text or symbolic environments (`ALFWorld`, `BabyAI`, `TextCraft` in [`asynchronous-system1-harness-evolution`](../skills/asynchronous-system1-harness-evolution/SKILL.md)), freezing the `27B` `JEV` backbone and evolving external Python/text `state`/`proc`/`wm` modules suffices (`0.08–0.58 s`). In continuous 3D visual streams (`ZZZ`, `Wukong`), however, text prompts and Python behaviour trees **cannot fix upstream visual discrimination errors** (`0.214` base `enemy_posture` accuracy; `0` clears on `3` unseen `Hollow Zero` bosses)—requiring targeted `LoRA` updates on failure-mined visual crops while keeping action-routing logic in an explicit behaviour tree $\phi$.
2. **Mandatory evaluation hygiene for dual `(weights θ, controller φ)` RSI loops**:
   - **Episode-disjoint splits**: Split train, checkpoint-selection validation, and reported test sets strictly by **independent full episodes** (never frame-subsampling from shared episodes or reporting accuracy on the checkpoint-selection split).
   - **2×2 Factorial attribution**: Whenever an outer coach modifies both $\theta$ (`LoRA`) and $\phi$ (behaviour-tree thresholds/cooldowns), run $(\theta_{\text{old}}, \phi_{\text{new}})$ and $(\theta_{\text{new}}, \phi_{\text{old}})$ on matched episode seeds before claiming model-level muscle-memory gains, and gate persistent promotion on *"DO: Pass every self-modification through one acceptance gate"* ([`recursive-improvement.md`](../rules/recursive-improvement.md)).
   - **Empirical concurrency measurement for staggered multi-lane inference**: Staggering $K = 3$ parallel System-1 request lanes reduces decision interval from $L$ to $L/3$ **only** if the serving GPU sustains $K$ concurrent forward passes without queueing or batch-latency inflation (`MASSys`, [`paired-serving-substrate-mas-benchmarking`](../skills/paired-serving-substrate-mas-benchmarking/SKILL.md)); always report measured wall-clock inter-action intervals `p50`/`p95` under load rather than idealized `L/K` schedules.

## Core Concept

Publishes detailed process traces from AI scientist agents performing real research tasks. Unlike result-only benchmarks, these traces capture the full workflow: hypothesis generation, experiment design, data analysis, and paper writing. Enables trajectory-level evaluation of research agents.

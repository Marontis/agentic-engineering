# Recursive Criticality of AI Self-Improvement

> **Paper**: [Recursive Criticality of AI Self-Improvement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00137)
> **Praxis source**: src:2609-00137v1

## Why Not a Skill?

Theoretical dynamical model of AI R&D feedback systems, providing mathematical foundations rather than an execution procedure.

---

## Core Concept

Formulates recursive AI self-improvement as a dynamical feedback loop between AI capabilities and AI R&D tooling. Identifies measurable conditions that govern whether an improvement loop reaches runaway exponential capability growth (super-critical) or saturates into diminishing returns (sub-critical).

### Key Finding

Runaway self-improvement requires the product of recursive feedback strength and propagation fidelity to exceed the rate of increasing task difficulty. In practical software engineering domains, the rising complexity of remaining bugs acts as a strong natural damper, keeping RSI in sub-critical regime unless tooling fundamentally expands.

# The Assistant's Ideal Self: Eliciting Post-Training Values

> **Paper**: [The Assistant's Ideal Self](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00304)
> **Praxis source**: src:2609-00304v1

## Why Not a Skill?

Empirical investigation into LLM self-concept, value stability, and post-training psychology, rather than an engineering workflow.

---

## Core Concept

Post-training equips LLMs with a helpful, honest, and harmless persona, but leaves their persistent self-concept undefined. Through counterbalanced pairwise-choice tasks across 32 qualities adapted from psychological self-concept instruments, the authors measure whether models express stable preferences for their own character development.

### Key Finding

Across varied framings (free vs. costly self-improvement, self vs. other recipient), models consistently prioritize moral qualities (benevolence, truthfulness, integrity) over competence, capability, or self-preserving agency, reflecting deep alignment to 3H post-training norms.
